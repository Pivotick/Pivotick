Triage pane — the states that are not a table

Four of these are the ones that decide whether the feature feels honest. None of them is an apology, and none of them is a silent truncation: a refusal always names the number, the limit and the way forward.

T0fetching
⌄Correlations
Fetching candidates…
The tab is created when fetch starts, not when results land (C3). That is what lets the analyst close the menu without cancelling the run — and it gives a failed fetch somewhere to live.
T4fetch failed
⌄Correlations
Couldn't fetch candidates.
Nothing was staged. Retrying runs the same request with the narrowing you already chose.
Retry keeps the narrowing. Losing it would send the analyst back through the gate they just cleared.
T5ceiling refusal
⌄Oversized
The source returned 14,203 candidates, over the 10,000 limit.
Nothing was staged — narrow and run again.
This is a refusal, not a truncation (D17). Showing the first 10,000 would be the one thing this whole design exists to prevent: a set that claims to be complete and isn't.
T2all deduped
⌄Correlations
All 210 are already on the canvas — nothing to triage
Their data was left untouched (D23). This is a normal outcome, not a failed run.
The success tone is deliberate. A shrink from ~210 advertised to 0 ingestable is exactly what dedup is for (D10).
T3zero results
⌄Correlations
No candidates came back
The narrowing may be tighter than the data.
Never phrased as an error, never "0 results found". Zero is an answer.
T6triage finished
⌄Correlations
Nothing left to triage
12 ingested · 198 rejected
The tally stays until the pane is closed, so the analyst can see what they just did — and so "reject all remaining" has a visible consequence.

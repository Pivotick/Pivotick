Origin-less pivots — the quiet second door

Search, import and staging pivots run with no selection at all (D19), so they cannot live in a selection-driven list. Proposal: they need no new chrome. They occupy the same Pivots surface — alone when nothing is selected, and folded into a collapsed group at the bottom when something is. One registry, one pipeline, one triage pane, exactly as D19 argued.

C14 No mainheader pill, no rail mode, no new surface. The brief asks for one proposal and asks it to stay quiet. A mainheader entry would give a secondary door the most prominent chrome in the app; a rail mode is the consumer's to ship, never the library's (plugin-rail-modes.md, and brief §5). Reusing the Pivots surface keeps D19's argument intact — the gap was never "the data came from elsewhere", it was "there is no node to run this on".
